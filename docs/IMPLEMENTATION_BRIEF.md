# FULL COLOR ATLAS — CLEAN REBUILD DIRECTIVE

This brief deliberately supersedes the old ChatGPT-clone-first direction and any prior six-scene mandatory-museum implementation. Preserve the old branches but build the new application from `main` on `rebuild/full-color-atlas-2026-10-28`. Do not import old game logic. The master spec at `docs/ATLAS_MASTER_INSTRUCTIONS.md` is the author's intellectual and visitor-experience authority; the Atlas-first product structure here resolves the fact that the master document still uses the historical "Living Notebook" heading.

## Identity and information hierarchy

**Product:** THE FULL COLOR ATLAS (by LBB / Language Beyond Binary).
**Venue:** an improvisational interactive museum of language and knowledge.
**Primary interaction:** a visitor's question, answered directly and expanded into relevant scenes and arguments when helpful.
**Guide:** the Exhibit Cart, an animated gold character with wit and intellectual humility.
**Supporting methods:** Refraction, Connection, Cross-Examination, Translation, Imagination, Metacognition, Human Judgment.
**Visitor artifact:** My Living Notebook; optional, saved locally by explicit user action.
**Optional wings:** Interdisciplinary Archive, Banter Bots, Reality Simulation, Cross-Examination, Research and Real-World Impact.
**Universal research doorway:** Read the White Paper and the canonical Open Invitation, never mandatory reading.

## Minimum finished visitor loop

1. Visitor arrives at the interactive Atlas, not a dashboard or a long lesson. The Exhibit Cart welcomes them and asks a meaningful question. Prompt suggestions rotate; visitors can ask anything.
2. Visitor enters either a broad question or an ordinary observation. The Cart answers in conversation.
3. The Atlas highlights plausible disciplinary lenses **as invitations, not claims of evidence**. Visitor can explore history, law, economics, physics, language, ethics, etc.
4. Visitor can summon fictional BB disagreements, cross-examine the Cart, or construct a hypothetical simulation; they can always request a direct answer.
5. Visitor saves/revises a self-authored note; revisions keep their original text; none become verified just because AI phrased them.
6. Visitor can access the paper, challenge the Atlas's own claims, inspect pledge status, and leave freely.

## What must not be fabricated

- No direct quotations without real verification; no fake literature sources.
- No unpublished full white paper: show the actual outline with accurate status until the paper exists.
- No charity, tax-deductible donation route, disbursement ledger, governance entity, submitted study, public participant program, proof of learning gains, or accepted partnership unless real records exist.
- No simulated BB as real independent experts.
- No entire source manuscript or private ChatGPT archives claimed ingested.
- No activation of OpenAI billing and public launch without explicit owner approval.

## Authoritative pledge terms

The intended commitment is **at least 50% of the creator's qualifying personal share of profits from defined related activities**, conditional on sustainability and finalized accounting/governance conditions. The story may finance the question; it may not purchase the answer. Neither half of every retail sale nor an operational charity should be implied.

## Accessibility

A readable dark, low-glare default; user-selectable high contrast and larger text; keyboard and screen-reader navigation; reduced-motion compatibility; no essential information encoded by color alone; no compulsory audio, animation, or visualization. The Atlas must teach in language even without visual spectacle.

## Definition of done for a first public beta

A visitor can open a single URL, ask a fresh question, receive a responsive live AI answer through the secured server API, explore a useful connection, save or revise a note, find the free research materials, understand what is proposed versus verified, and leave without registering or buying. The preview implementation here has all UI affordances but **does not yet satisfy public live launch readiness**.
