Create a beautiful responsive interface with:

**CENTER — Conversation**

- Natural, flowing chat with the Exhibit Cart.
- Readable message history and streaming responses.
- Suggested questions for new visitors.
- A persistent text composer.
- Ability to start a new conversation and revisit saved conversations.

**LEFT SIDEBAR — Navigation**

- New Conversation
- Recent Conversations
- Explore the Full Color Atlas
- Explore A Trial of Color
- Cross-Examination
- My Notebook
- Sources and Research

On mobile, use an accessible collapsible menu.

**RIGHT PANEL — The Living Notebook**

A collapsible notebook that follows the conversation and any optional journeys.

Allow visitors to write and save:

- My Theory
- My Anti-Theory
- Evidence
- Objections
- Questions
- What Would Change My Mind?
- Revised Conclusions

Preserve original entries and track revisions. Do not automatically save speculative AI statements as verified evidence.
